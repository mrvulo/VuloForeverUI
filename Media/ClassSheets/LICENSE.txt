Vulo Fantasy class sheets (vulofantasy1.tga, vulofantasy2.tga)
==============================================================

The class glyphs used in these sheets are from game-icons.net and are
licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0).
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Icons made by Lorc (https://lorcblog.blogspot.com) and Delapouite
(https://delapouite.com). Available on https://game-icons.net

The glyphs were recoloured and composited onto class-coloured tiles for
VuloClassicUI.

vuloepic.tga is original artwork created by the addon author (Vulo) and
is not from game-icons.net.

Glyphs used
-----------
Vulo Fantasy 1: crossed-swords, fireball, plain-dagger, paw, dragon-head,
  bow-arrow, lightning-trio, prayer, imp-laugh, winged-shield, grim-reaper,
  fist, devil-mask.
Vulo Fantasy 2: crossed-swords, wizard-staff, daggers, oak-leaf,
  dragon-spiral, high-shot, lightning-arc, holy-symbol, evil-minion,
  thor-hammer, skull-signet, high-kick, horned-helm.
