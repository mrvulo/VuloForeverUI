## 0.26.0

**New**

- **Quality of Life** — fishing: with the line out, Ctrl + your fishing key
  casts again at once, without touching pole or lure.
- **Quality of Life** — fishing: bound gear with a Fishing bonus (hat, boots,
  gloves …) goes on with the fishing key and comes off with your weapons
  when you mount. The pole with the highest bonus is picked first.
- **Quality of Life** — fishing: a sound when the fish got away or nothing
  was on the hook yet, and on request a reminder when you reeled in and did
  not cast again. Pick the sound in the options.
- **Quality of Life** — fishing: a session count per character (casts,
  catches, catch rate, catches per hour, time spent fishing) on the options
  page, and a short catch summary above the middle of the screen.
- **Quality of Life** — fishing: set the fishing key in a small window that
  takes the next key you press, see your Fishing skill, loot fishing catches
  automatically, get your weapons back when you mount, and a chat line when
  a skill tier is full.
- **Bags** — currencies you tick as "Show on Backpack" in the game's currency
  window now show in the bag header too, before the ones picked in the
  options.

**Changed**

- **Flight time** — exact flight times from the game's own flight paths, also
  for a route you have never flown before. No more "~" estimates.
- **Cooldown Manager** — a row added with an older spell rank now shows your
  highest known rank: icon, cooldown, charges, glow, active phase and key
  binding all follow it. Adding the spell again keeps the row in its place.
- **Quality of Life** — fishing: "Hear the bobber better" now turns other
  sounds down and stops distance from muffling the splash while the line is
  out.

**Fixed**

- **Bags** — the gold list showed every character twice, once without a
  realm name, and counted both in the total.
- **Quality of Life** — the fishing key hint showed an empty box instead of
  an arrow.
