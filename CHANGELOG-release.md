## 0.20.1

**Changed**

- **Edit Mode** — window positions are no longer written into Blizzard's Edit
  Mode layout. They still apply on top of it as before; "Reset this frame"
  returns a frame to the place in Blizzard's layout.

**Fixed**

- **Unit Frames** — party and raid frames no longer throw thousands of errors
  after closing an Edit Mode, most visibly in a group.
- **Unit Frames** — Classic style: the damage number on your portrait is
  centred again on German, Spanish, Portuguese and Russian clients.
