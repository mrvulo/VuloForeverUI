## 0.27.0

**New**

- **Quality of Life** — loot roll list: group loot rolls can show as one
  compact list instead of the game's roll windows. Each row has the item,
  a slim timer bar and the Need, Greed (or Transmog), Disenchant and Pass
  buttons, with how many players picked each choice. Hover a row to see
  everyone's choice and roll, and the winner shows in the bar at the end.
  The header bar folds the list or hides it until the next roll, and you
  can drag the list anywhere by its header. Width, height, text size,
  spacing, grow direction and bar texture are in the options, with test
  rolls to try the look. Off by default; switching it off again brings the
  game's roll windows back after a reload.
- **Quality of Life** — fishing: the fishing key can now be a mouse wheel
  direction, with or without Shift, Ctrl or Alt. One turn of the wheel
  casts once and does not reel straight back in.
