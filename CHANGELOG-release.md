## 0.25.0

**New**

- **Quality of Life** — the game's own windows can be moved: drag the
  character window, spellbook, professions, map, merchant, mail, auction
  house, friends, collections, achievements and the others by the title
  bar, and each opens in that place from then on. A protected window
  cannot be moved during a fight and shows in its own place until the
  fight is over. "Reset window positions" brings them all back.
- **Bags** — profession bags as sections of their own: a reagent bag, herb
  bag, quiver or other bag for one kind of thing leaves the categories and
  the "all bags" block and stands underneath, slot by slot, under the bag's
  name. A button in the tool row folds these sections away; a search
  unfolds them. Can be switched off.

**Changed**

- **Chat** — the lines kept across a reload are now kept per tab, and each
  tab gets its own lines back, above the lines of the new session. "How
  many lines" counts per tab, so a busy channel no longer pushes your
  whispers out.

**Fixed**

- **Chat** — whispers from a tab of their own were gone after logging back
  in: they came back in the first tab or not at all.
- **Chat** — changing a chat setting no longer wipes the restored lines for
  the rest of the session.
- **Quality of Life** — the day's gold "Balance" showed the whole purse
  instead of earned minus spent.
- **Bags** — a character that logged in without moving any money was listed
  with no gold in the money tooltip, and "This session" counted the whole
  purse as a gain.
