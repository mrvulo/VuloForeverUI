## 0.21.0

**New**

- **Unit Frames** — on an enemy target, the buffs your character can take
  off (Purge, Dispel Magic, Devour Magic) glow; all other buffs stay as they
  are, and friendly targets are not changed. Standard and Classic style, for
  characters who can dispel magic ("Buffs you can dispel glow").
- **Bags** — the keyring is a foldable section at the bottom of the bag
  window, with a key button in the tool row; the game's own keyring button
  opens it too ("Show the keyring").
- **Bags** — "Clear the character list" removes every other character from
  the account-wide gold list, for example those of realms that no longer
  exist.
- **Cooldown Manager** — talent conditions per icon: right-click an icon in
  the preview and pick talents it needs or must not have. With a build that
  does not match, the icon leaves the bar and the bar closes up.
- **Cooldown Manager** — "Show the time left on the target" puts the
  remaining time of your own DoT on its icon; it turns green once a recast
  no longer cuts off a tick.
- **Quality of Life** — new Professions tab: with Mining or Herbalism the
  game marks ore and herbs in front of you with its interaction icon, and
  the nearest one sparkles (range and colour adjustable).
- **Quality of Life** — at a merchant, click the number in the split window
  to type an amount; Enter buys or splits exactly that many.
- **Skins** — the friends list in the dark Modern style, and a third style
  "Cards" for the levelling guide window.

**Changed**

- **Quality of Life** — "Max" in the merchant's split window now enters one
  full stack; shift-click "Max" still takes as many as gold, stock and bag
  space allow.

**Fixed**

- **Chat** — item links, player names and quests in the chat can be clicked
  again.
- **Chat** — the channel name in front of the input line ("Guild:") has the
  same font and size as the text you type, and the text cursor sits at the
  end of a long line.
- **Bags** — using items from the bags and buying a bank slot no longer get
  blocked after a visit to the bank.
- **Cooldown Manager** — a spell no longer shows twice in the preview after
  you learn a new rank.
- **Damage meter** — values below 1 per second no longer show a long row of
  decimals.
