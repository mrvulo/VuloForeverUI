## 0.24.0

**New**

- **Quality of Life** — macro factory, a new "Macros" tab: tick a macro and
  the addon writes it into your character's macro list and keeps it up to
  date, so the button always uses the best item of its kind that is in
  your bags and that your level allows. Food, drink, healthstone, healing
  potion and mana potion for everyone; soulstone, spellstone and firestone
  for warlocks; mana gem for mages. Food and drink that give a buff of
  their own come last.
- **Quality of Life** — the warlock and mage macros create or conjure the
  item with the same button when none is in your bags.
- **Quality of Life** — "onto the cursor" next to each macro picks it up so
  you can drop it on an action bar. Macros are rewritten out of combat
  only; unticking a macro deletes it.
