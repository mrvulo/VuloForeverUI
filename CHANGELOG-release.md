## 0.16.0

**New**

- **Quality of Life** — the gold check is in the right-click menu on a
  player: on the target, party and raid frames, and on a chat name while
  that player is in reach. A click scans them and opens the window.
- **Skins** — the character window shows your average item level in the
  level strip, beside your level. Coloured by the quality of your gear or
  in a colour of your own.

**Changed**

- **Unit Frames** — the Classic and Standard styles can show the game's own
  threat percentage above the target and focus frames.

**Fixed**

- **Auras** — on rounded buffs and debuffs the cooldown swipe now covers the
  icon right up to the ring instead of leaving a bright rim. The same for
  the rounded trinket slots in Quality of Life.
